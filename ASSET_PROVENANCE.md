# Asset provenance

Krabville's production artwork was commissioned and generated for Canadaverse,
then mechanically normalized for consistent frame sizes, transparency,
nearest-neighbour rendering, and bounded transfer size. Canadaverse grants use
of these included assets with this Apache-2.0 project.

Production hashes and byte sizes are recorded in
`frontend/public/assets/manifest.json`. Scene-critical map and resident artwork
totals about 2.65 MB; the larger Season One poster is loaded only from the
archive.
